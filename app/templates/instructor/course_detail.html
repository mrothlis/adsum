📥 Export for D2L Gradebook
How to put this into the D2L gradebook
  1. Click Export for D2L Gradebook above and save the CSV.
  2. In D2L: Grades → Import, choose the file, and tick “Create new grade item when an unrecognized item is referenced”.
  3. On New Grade Item Properties, set Maximum Points to 100. D2L defaults this to 10, which would show every student as 1000%.
  4. Continue through the preview, then click Import.

Scores are the percentage of sessions attended. Anyone not on the D2L classlist is skipped as an “unrecognized user” — that row is safe to ignore.
