How to put this into the D2L gradebook
  1. Click Export for D2L Gradebook above and save the CSV.
  2. In D2L: Grades → Import, choose the file, and tick “Create new grade item when an unrecognized item is referenced”.
  3. On New Grade Item Properties, set Maximum Points to 100 for Attendance. D2L defaults this to 10, which would show every student as 1000%. Attendance Detail is a Text item and needs no properties.
  4. Continue through the preview, then click Import.

The export carries two columns: Attendance, the numeric grade, and Attendance Detail, a text column holding every class date and its status in one cell — so D2L keeps the full record without one column per meeting. Text items are not counted in the final grade. Because that column holds the whole history, a D2L grade export is a complete backup — Restore from D2L rebuilds this app from it, so D2L stays the system of record. Scores are the percentage of sessions attended, after the first {{ allowed_absences }} unexcused absences are forgiven. Excused absences are dropped from the count entirely. Anyone not on the D2L classlist is skipped as an “unrecognized user” — that row is safe to ignore.
