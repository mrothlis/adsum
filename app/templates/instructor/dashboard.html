No courses yet. That is normal on day one.

Adsum does not have a Create Course button, on purpose. A course appears here the first time you open the Adsum link from inside that course in D2L. D2L tells Adsum the course name, the code and who you are, so nothing has to be typed twice.

Do this once per course:

  1. In D2L, open the course.
  2. Go to Content.
  3. Choose Add Existing Activities, then External Learning Tool.
  4. Add the Adsum link.
  5. Click the link once. Come back here and the course will be listed.
