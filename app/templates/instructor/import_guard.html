What to check first

  1. In D2L, open Classlist for this course and export it again. A file exported from another course, another section, or a previous term looks perfectly valid here.
  2. Make sure no search or filter was active when you exported. A filtered export only contains the students you were looking at, and every other student reads as a drop.
  3. Check that you picked the Classlist export and not the Grades export.
