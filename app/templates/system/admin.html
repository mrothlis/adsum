D2L Brightspace connection

Shared by every course on this installation. Changing the key or secret breaks every instructor's D2L launches until the matching values are updated in D2L too. Change both sides together, then click a launch to confirm.

Leave a field blank to keep its current value. Launch URL: {{ base_url }}/lti/launch

Sharing this with several instructors

Anyone holding the consumer key and secret can forge a launch as any user, so handing them to every colleague is the weak point in a shared install. The cleaner arrangement is to have IT register Adsum once as an org-level External Learning Tool in D2L. Instructors can then add the link to their own courses without ever seeing the secret.
